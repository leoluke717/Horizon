## [China Issues Mandatory Standard for Autonomous Driving Safety Baseline](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-04/10672042.shtml) ⭐️ 9.0/10

China&\#x27;s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology released and approved the mandatory national standard &\#x27;Intelligent Connected Vehicles – Autonomous Driving System Safety Requirements&\#x27; \(GB 44721—2026\), which sets a unified safety baseline for autonomous driving products and will take effect on July 1, 2027. This standard is China&\#x27;s first mandatory national standard for L3 and L4 autonomous driving systems, providing a clear regulatory framework that will guide development, enhance safety, and boost industry confidence. The standard applies to M \(passenger\) and N \(cargo\) vehicles equipped with L3 and L4 systems, excluding automatic parking systems. It upgrades a 2024 recommended standard to mandatory, covering enterprise lifecycle safety assurance, system dynamic driving capability, human-machine interaction and user notification, and multi-dimensional inspection and testing.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 4, 14:20

**Background**: China&\#x27;s driving automation classification standard defines L3 as conditional automation where the system handles all dynamic driving tasks under specific conditions but requires human takeover when requested, and L4 as high automation where the system operates without human intervention within its design domain. Mandatory national standards are legally binding, unlike recommended standards, and this is China&\#x27;s first mandatory standard for autonomous driving systems.

## [South Korea passes law abolishing prosecution&\#x27;s investigative powers](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gj/2026/08-05/10672054.shtml) ⭐️ 9.0/10

South Korea&\#x27;s government, presided over by President Lee Jae-myung, approved an amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act that completely abolishes the prosecution&\#x27;s direct and supplementary investigation powers. This marks a fundamental restructuring of the criminal justice system that has been in place for over 70 years. This reform significantly reduces the prosecution&\#x27;s power and shifts investigative authority to the police, potentially altering the balance of power in South Korea&\#x27;s criminal justice system. It could affect how crimes are investigated and prosecuted, and may lead to more checks and balances. The amendment was passed at a cabinet meeting on August 4, 2026, and it eliminates both direct investigation and supplementary investigation powers of the prosecution. The implementation timeline and transitional details have not been specified.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 国际 · Aug 4, 21:38

**Background**: In South Korea, the prosecution has historically held strong investigative powers, including the ability to directly investigate crimes and to supplement police investigations. This system has been criticized for concentrating too much power in the prosecution, leading to calls for reform. The amendment represents a major shift towards a more police-led investigative process, similar to systems in many other democracies.

## [Samsung Bans Smart TV Apps with Residential Proxy SDKs](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=84999) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Samsung has announced a ban on smart TV apps that embed residential proxy SDKs, and will remove such apps from its Tizen app store. This follows a similar policy change by LG. This move protects user privacy by preventing smart TVs from being used as nodes in residential proxy networks without users&\#x27; knowledge. It also impacts app developers and residential proxy service providers who relied on this practice. Previous research found that over 42% of LG webOS apps and more than a quarter of Samsung Tizen apps contained residential proxy SDKs. Some of these apps had hundreds of millions of installs and were even recommended by Samsung.

rss · Solidot · Aug 4, 06:45

**Background**: A residential proxy is a proxy server that routes internet traffic through IP addresses assigned by ISPs to real residential devices, making the traffic appear as genuine user connections. A residential proxy SDK is a software library bundled into an app that enrolls the user&\#x27;s device and home IP address into a commercial proxy network. Smart TVs are particularly attractive for this purpose because they are always powered on and connected to home WiFi, yet users rarely monitor their background activity. By banning these SDKs, Samsung aims to curb the unauthorized use of users&\#x27; home IP addresses for proxy services.

## [Google builds $200B financing machine for Anthropic](https://www.ft.com/content/549f2e23-5aa2-49c7-9ea6-a9784ab7087c) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google has quietly constructed one of the largest infrastructure financing structures ever, worth approximately $200 billion, to deliver AI chips and computing power to Anthropic. The first tranche of $35 billion in hardware, including 1 million TPUs, was already transacted in June via a special purpose vehicle called Compute SPV. This marks a structural shift in AI capital allocation, enabling a startup without a credit rating to access massive computing resources while spreading risk across multiple financial and technology partners. The vendor-financing model, borrowed from aerospace and industrial sectors, could become a template for funding future AI infrastructure at unprecedented scale. The structure involves multiple parties: Google guarantees data centers, Broadcom purchases and helps finance chips, while Apollo and Blackstone buy hardware and lease it back to Anthropic. The total contract value is about $200 billion, with roughly 80% directly tied to chips, and the first Compute SPV transaction equates to 1 gigawatt of computing power.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 4, 10:52

**Background**: TPU \(Tensor Processing Unit\) is a custom ASIC developed by Google specifically for accelerating machine learning tasks, offering 15-30x performance over contemporary GPUs in neural network inference. A Special Purpose Vehicle \(SPV\) is a legal entity created for a specific financial transaction, allowing risks and assets to be isolated from the parent companies. Vendor financing, commonly used by Boeing and GE, involves the manufacturer or a third party providing loans or leases to help customers purchase expensive equipment, keeping the assets off the buyer&\#x27;s balance sheet.

The Ministry of Education released the &\#x27;Learning by Doing&\#x27; Leading Action Guide for Science Education in Compulsory Education on August 4, 2026. It requires students in grades 4-9 to complete at least one scientific inquiry task per semester starting from the fall of 2026, with each task lasting at least 4 class hours. This policy shifts science education from passive learning to hands-on inquiry, emphasizing real-world problem solving and interdisciplinary learning. It integrates scientific inquiry into the comprehensive quality evaluation system, potentially changing how science is taught and assessed in Chinese schools. The guide provides six inquiry themes: life health, ecological environment, earth mysteries, aerospace, emerging industries, and artificial intelligence. Evaluation uses a documentary method recording students&\#x27; problem awareness, practical and cooperation abilities, without additional exams.
