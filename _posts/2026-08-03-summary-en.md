## [EU Ends De Minimis Exemption, Imposes €3 Duty on Low-Value Imports](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-02/10670951.shtml) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On July 1, 2026, the European Union eliminated the de minimis exemption for imports valued under €150, imposing a temporary uniform tariff of €3 per item during a two-year transition period, in addition to existing VAT. This regulatory change disrupts the low-price direct shipping model of Chinese e-commerce platforms like Temu and Shein, which depended on the duty-free threshold to keep costs low. It could force a shift from volume-driven, low-margin sales to brand-building and higher pricing strategies, affecting millions of cross-border transactions and EU consumers. The €3 per-item tariff applies to all commercial parcels entering the EU, regardless of value, during the two-year transition period. For goods priced around €10, the tariff represents a 30% cost increase, significantly eroding profit margins.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 2, 13:48

**Background**: The EU&\#x27;s de minimis rule previously exempted imports valued at or below €150 from customs duties, allowing low-value parcels to enter duty-free. This policy benefited Chinese cross-border e-commerce platforms that shipped small, inexpensive items directly to EU consumers. The new regulation, effective July 1, 2026, ends this exemption and introduces a €3 per-item uniform tariff during a two-year transition period, with VAT still applicable. The change aims to curb the influx of cheap Chinese imports and protect local retailers.

## [China Expands Housing Provident Fund Withdrawal and Coverage](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-02/10670745.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The State Council has approved a draft amendment to the Housing Provident Fund Management Regulations. The amendment aims to broaden the withdrawal and use scope, expand coverage, and enhance management efficiency to better meet diverse housing consumption needs. This move is significant because it could make housing more affordable for millions of workers by allowing more flexible use of provident funds. It also signals a policy shift towards supporting housing consumption and potentially stimulating the real estate market. The specific provisions and effective date of the amendment have not been detailed yet. The draft still needs further review and final approval before implementation.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 2, 02:24

**Background**: The Housing Provident Fund is a mandatory savings scheme for housing in China, where employees and employers contribute a percentage of salary. It was established to provide affordable housing loans and support homeownership. However, critics have pointed out that the fund&\#x27;s usage is often restricted, and many low-income workers find it difficult to access. The amendment aims to address these issues by expanding the scope of withdrawals and coverage.

## [Apple Limits Bug Report Submissions to Curb AI-Generated Low-Quality Reports](https://www.ft.com/content/4532122d-90f2-4433-9df6-ca99d8a141d2?syn-25a6b1a6=1) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple has limited the number of vulnerability reports researchers can submit simultaneously and introduced a 30-day cooldown period, starting June 2025, to address the influx of low-quality security reports generated by AI models. This change could hinder legitimate security researchers from reporting critical vulnerabilities, as demonstrated by Bynario&\#x27;s discovery of over 50 macOS flaws including a privilege escalation chain, which they could not report due to the limit. It highlights the growing tension between AI-assisted security research and the need for quality control in bug bounty programs. Apple has contacted Bynario and reviewed their submissions, while also using AI tools from Anthropic and OpenAI to enhance its own security defenses. The recent system security update fixed about five times the usual number of vulnerabilities, crediting AI-assisted discovery.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 2, 05:50

**Background**: Bug bounty programs encourage security researchers to report vulnerabilities in exchange for rewards. However, the rise of AI-generated security reports has led to a flood of low-quality submissions that overwhelm human reviewers. Apple&\#x27;s new rules aim to filter out such noise, but may also impact researchers who legitimately use AI tools to find vulnerabilities. The incident with Bynario illustrates the potential for AI to discover serious flaws, yet be constrained by submission limits.
