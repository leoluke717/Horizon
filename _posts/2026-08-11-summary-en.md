## [Anthropic Adds Invisible Watermarks to Claude-Generated Text](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=85063) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Anthropic has deployed invisible, machine-readable watermarks on text generated by its Claude AI models to comply with the EU AI Act&\#x27;s Article 50 transparency requirements. The watermarks are imperceptible to humans but can be detected by specialized tools, and they persist even when text is copied and pasted. This move marks a concrete step by a major AI company to meet binding regulatory requirements, setting a precedent for transparency in AI-generated content. It affects all Claude users globally, not just in Europe, and could influence how other AI providers implement similar measures. Anthropic uses different watermarking techniques for text and files: text receives an invisible watermark, while files include signed source information. The company expects that some watermarks will remain effective even after editing.

rss · Solidot · Aug 11, 13:48

**Background**: The EU AI Act, particularly Article 50, requires providers of AI systems to ensure that AI-generated content is detectable and labeled. Invisible watermarking is a technique that embeds a hidden pattern in text that is imperceptible to humans but can be identified by software. This is similar to methods used for images and videos, but for text it is more challenging because text has less redundancy. Anthropic&\#x27;s implementation aims to balance transparency with user experience, as the watermarks do not affect readability.

## [Anthropic to Add AI Watermarks to Claude Content by 2026](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Anthropic has signed the EU AI Act code of conduct for AI-generated content transparency, committing to embed machine-readable watermarks and C2PA provenance metadata in Claude models starting August 2, 2026. This commitment marks a major step toward AI transparency and compliance with the EU AI Act, potentially influencing global standards for identifying AI-generated content. The text watermarks will be invisible and machine-readable, while supported files will use C2PA provenance metadata. Detection of a watermark only suggests possible Claude processing, and its absence does not prove content is not AI-generated.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 11, 03:06

**Background**: The EU AI Act is a comprehensive regulation governing artificial intelligence, including requirements for transparency of AI-generated content. Machine-readable watermarks are digital signals embedded in content that can be detected by systems. The C2PA \(Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity\) standard cryptographically binds provenance metadata to media files, providing a verifiable record of a digital asset&\#x27;s origin and history.
