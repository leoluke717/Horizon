## [Guangzhou Court Orders Evergrande Real Estate Group Into Bankruptcy Liquidation](https://weibo.com/1642585887/5334339212283916) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 21, the Guangzhou Intermediate People&\#x27;s Court ruled to accept the bankruptcy liquidation case of Evergrande Real Estate Group, the main domestic real estate entity of China Evergrande. As of the end of 2022, the company had total assets of 1.47 trillion yuan and total liabilities of 1.83 trillion yuan. This is one of the largest corporate bankruptcies in China&\#x27;s real estate sector and signals a major contraction of capital and a structural shift in the industry. Creditors, homebuyers, employees, and the broader property market will be affected, and the actual recovery rate for creditors is expected to be very low. The court&\#x27;s decision was based on severe insolvency and a perceived lack of restructuring value; liquidation is intended to fix the debt scale. Auditors had issued a disclaimer of opinion on the company&\#x27;s financial statements, and asset realization values remain uncertain and dependent on market conditions.

**Background**: Bankruptcy liquidation is a legal process in which a court oversees the sale of a debtor&\#x27;s assets to repay creditors, typically used when a company is insolvent and has no viable restructuring path. A disclaimer of opinion in an audit means the auditor could not obtain sufficient evidence to form an opinion on the financial statements, often due to severe uncertainty or scope limitations. The recovery rate in bankruptcy measures the percentage of claims creditors actually receive from liquidated assets; in Evergrande&\#x27;s case, industry insiders expect it to be extremely low because asset sale values depend on market conditions.
