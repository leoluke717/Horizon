The facility spans 120,000 square meters, supports million-GPU parallel computing, and has a planned capacity of 2GW with over 80% green electricity. This facility significantly expands China&\#x27;s AI computing capacity, potentially reducing training costs and enabling larger-scale AI models. Its high green energy usage and location in a national computing hub align with China&\#x27;s East-West Computing Transfer project and sustainability goals. ## [China Tightens Social Security Base Enforcement, Seeks Balance](https://weekly.caixin.com/2026-08-07/102472223.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tax authorities in China are now cross-referencing individual income tax and social security data to enforce accurate contribution bases. Multiple regions have set consolidation targets of 65-100% for this year and require full compliance within three to five years. This enforcement significantly raises labor costs for many enterprises, particularly those that previously underreported contribution bases, potentially affecting employment and economic stability. It is also a key measure to shore up social security funds and address the growing pension deficit. The transfer of social security collection to tax authorities began in 2019, but enforcement is now accelerating. Regions vary in their targets and provide transition periods, while some companies are offsetting higher costs through salary cuts or outsourcing.

**Background**: In China, social security contributions are based on a percentage of an employee&\#x27;s salary, but many companies have historically underreported these bases to reduce costs. The government has been working to consolidate bases to ensure adequate funding for the social security system, which faces pressure from an aging population. The transfer of collection to tax authorities in 2019 was a step toward better enforcement.

## [Paris mandates helmets and reflective gear for e-scooters](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260808-paris-orders-e-scooter-users-to-wear-helmets-reflective-gear) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Paris and its suburbs have implemented a new regulation requiring all users of motorized personal transport devices such as e-scooters, hoverboards, and self-balancing scooters to wear helmets and reflective gear. Violators face fines of €135 for not wearing a helmet and €35 for not wearing reflective gear. This regulation comes in response to a sharp increase in accidents involving e-scooters, with deaths in France rising from 45 to 79 in 2025. It highlights growing concerns over e-scooter safety and may influence similar policies in other cities. The regulation applies to all motorized personal transport devices on public roads at all times. Madrid banned rental e-scooters in 2024, and Brussels announced a similar ban in June 2025.

**Background**: Electric scooters and similar devices have become common in urban areas, but their use has led to a rise in accidents.
