## [Jilin establishes 100 billion yuan rural revitalization fund pool](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-13/10677150.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

This initiative provides substantial financial support for rural revitalization in Jilin, potentially boosting agricultural development and rural incomes. The favorable credit terms could greatly improve access to financing for farmers and rural businesses, addressing a key bottleneck in rural economic growth. The fund pool is specifically for &\#x27;rich people industry&\#x27; \(富民产业\), focusing on industries that benefit rural residents. The &\#x27;whole village credit&\#x27; model involves banks assessing creditworthiness at the village level, allowing households to access loans based on collective village credit with specific caps and terms as announced.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 13, 11:04

**Background**: Rural revitalization is a key national strategy in China aimed at developing rural areas. Access to credit has been a major challenge for farmers and rural enterprises due to lack of collateral and credit history. &\#x27;Whole village credit&\#x27; is a model where banks evaluate the creditworthiness of an entire village, enabling collective access to loans with favorable terms. The &\#x27;rich people industry&\#x27; fund pool is a targeted financial instrument to support industries that directly benefit rural populations.
