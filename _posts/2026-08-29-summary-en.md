## [French Court Rules Cosmic Radiation Caused Flight Attendant&\#x27;s Breast Cancer](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=85220) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A French court ruled for the first time that cosmic radiation was an occupational factor in former Air France flight attendant Sophie Lainault&\#x27;s breast cancer, alongside passive smoking and night work. The decision creates a legal precedent for recognizing aircrew cancers as occupational diseases. It also pressures airlines, insurers, and occupational-disease compensation systems to account for radiation-related cancer risks among aircrew. Lainault, 59, flew 12,600 hours for Air France from 1989 to 2019, more than half on night shifts; Paris-origin long-haul flights often use polar routes, where Earth&\#x27;s magnetic shielding is weaker. A Harvard Medical School study published this month found that among more than 500 occupations, flight attendants and pilots had the highest radiation-related cancer mortality, at about 6.9% and 6.7% of deaths respectively.

rss · Solidot · Aug 28, 08:14

**Background**: Cosmic radiation is high-energy particles from space that increase with altitude, and polar routes allow more of it to reach aircraft because the Earth&\#x27;s magnetic field offers less protection near the poles. Occupational disease recognition usually requires proving a work-related cause; this ruling treats radiation, passive smoking, and night work as cumulative occupational factors. The case highlights growing scientific evidence linking long-haul flying to elevated cancer risk among aircrew.
