## [China Releases Enforcement Guide for Advertising Citations](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/fz/2026/08-12/10676094.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The &\#x27;Advertising Citation Content Enforcement Guide&\#x27; has been officially implemented, requiring advertisers to provide evidence for claims such as &\#x27;number one&\#x27; and banning hidden restrictions in fine print. This guide significantly tightens advertising compliance rules in China, protecting consumers from deceptive marketing and forcing advertisers to substantiate their claims with verifiable data. The guide defines &\#x27;advertising citation content&\#x27; and clarifies that advertisers bear the burden of proof for the truthfulness, accuracy, and legality of such citations. It targets common tactics like vague &\#x27;number one&\#x27; claims and hidden disclaimers.

**Background**: Advertising citation content refers to claims in ads that cite data, statistics, or rankings \(e.g., &\#x27;sales number one&\#x27;\). By the end of July 2026, 192 agricultural small and micro entities with an A-level or above 农安信用 \(agricultural quality credit\) rating had received a total credit line of 14.7 billion yuan, with 8.61 billion yuan actually disbursed. ## [South Korea&\#x27;s Top Court Orders Nippon Steel to Compensate Forced Laborers](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gj/2026/08-12/10676535.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

South Korea&\#x27;s Supreme Court has upheld a lower court ruling ordering Nippon Steel to pay 80 million won \(approximately 380,000 yuan\) in compensation to the families of Koreans forced into labor during World War II. This final ruling sets a legal precedent for similar historical labor claims, potentially affecting dozens of pending cases and straining diplomatic relations between South Korea and Japan. The ruling is legally binding and final, but enforcement may involve cross-border legal challenges as the defendant is a Japanese company.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 国际 · Aug 12, 12:26

**Background**: During Japan&\#x27;s colonial rule of Korea from 1910 to 1945, many Koreans were forcibly mobilized to work in Japanese factories and mines under harsh conditions. In 2018, South Korea&\#x27;s Supreme Court ruled that individual victims have the right to seek compensation, overturning previous agreements between the two governments. This latest decision reaffirms that stance and continues a series of legal battles over wartime reparations.

Hong Kong has officially launched its first compliant stablecoin, granting access to initial distributors and institutional investors under the new regulatory framework. This launch implements Hong Kong&\#x27;s stablecoin regulatory regime, creating a new regulated financial instrument that could enhance the city&\#x27;s role as a digital asset hub and provide a safe, compliant on-ramp for institutional participation. The stablecoin is issued under the Stablecoins Ordinance effective August 1, 2025, which requires issuers to hold a license, maintain minimum capital of HK$25 million paid-up share capital, and adhere to strict reserve and disclosure rules.

google\_news · 财新 · Aug 12, 11:12

**Background**: Hong Kong implemented a comprehensive regulatory regime for stablecoin issuers on August 1, 2025, under the Stablecoins Ordinance \(Cap. 566\). The regime requires licensing by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority \(HKMA\) and sets out requirements for reserves, capital, anti-money laundering, and disclosure. This launch represents the first compliant stablecoin product under this framework, allowing distributors and institutional investors to access a regulated stablecoin.
