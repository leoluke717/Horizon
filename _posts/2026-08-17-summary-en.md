## [Qwen3.8 27B Scores 52, Outperforms Larger Models](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Qwen3.8 27B, a 27-billion-parameter open-source model, achieved a score of 52 on the Artificial Analysis benchmark, matching the performance of DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731, a model with over 150 billion parameters, and outperforming all models in the 40B-150B range. This achievement demonstrates a dramatic reduction in the parameter count required for frontier-level AI performance, enabling high-quality inference on consumer hardware such as gaming PCs. It challenges the prevailing trend of scaling up model sizes and could reshape deployment strategies for AI developers and enterprises. Qwen3.8 27B is a dense hybrid-attention model with 27 billion parameters, supporting a 1 million token context window and requiring only 24.6 GiB of memory in FP8 format. It is a native vision-language model capable of understanding images and videos with flexible thinking control.

## [Waymo imports 3,200 Zeekr autonomous vehicles from China](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=85111) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Waymo has imported 3,200 Zeekr autonomous vehicles from China since 2024, including 2,600 in 2025 alone, despite a 127.5% US tariff. The vehicles, named Waymo Ojai, are significantly cheaper than the Jaguar I-Pace even after tariff and modifications. This move demonstrates a major capital allocation shift for Waymo, dramatically reducing per-vehicle costs and potentially accelerating the expansion of its robotaxi fleet. It also highlights the impact of US-China trade tariffs on the EV industry and could pressure competitors to adopt similar cost-saving strategies. The Zeekr-based Waymo Ojai is the sixth-generation robotaxi, featuring updated sensors and a 17-megapixel imager. After a 127.5% tariff, the vehicle costs about $86,500, plus $25,000 for hardware and software, totaling over $100,000, still less than half the cost of a modified Jaguar I-Pace.

**Background**: Waymo, a subsidiary of Alphabet, is a leading autonomous driving technology company that operates robotaxi services in multiple US cities. The company previously used modified Jaguar I-Pace vehicles, which cost over $200,000 each. Zeekr is a Chinese premium EV brand owned by Geely, founded in 2021. The Waymo Ojai, originally known as the Zeekr RT, is the first vehicle to use Waymo&\#x27;s sixth-generation driver system.

## [OpenCode Go Drastically Cuts DeepSeek Quotas, Flash Drops 94%](https://opencode.ai/docs/go/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenCode Go has significantly reduced the usage quotas for DeepSeek models on its platform. The typical quota for DeepSeek V4 Flash dropped from approximately 63,300 requests per 5 hours to 3,800, a 94% decrease, while the V4 Pro quota fell from about 3,450 to 1,050, a 70% decrease. This drastic reduction severely limits access for users who rely on OpenCode Go for affordable coding model subscriptions. It may push users to seek alternative services or directly use DeepSeek&\#x27;s API, and raises questions about the sustainability of low-cost access to powerful AI models. The new quotas are documented on OpenCode Go&\#x27;s official documentation page, but the previous quotas were not officially listed and come from community reports. The reduction applies to both the Flash and Pro variants of DeepSeek V4, with Flash being hit harder proportionally.

**Background**: OpenCode Go is a $10/month subscription service that provides access to various open-source coding models, including DeepSeek models. DeepSeek V4 Flash and Pro are large Mixture-of-Experts \(MoE\) language models with 284B and 1.6T total parameters respectively, supporting a 1M-token context window. The quota reduction represents a significant policy change that affects the value proposition of the OpenCode Go subscription.

## [Apple Ordered to Revise ATT Consent Pop-Ups After German Ruling](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-change-app-data-consent-rules-german-regulator-says-2026-08-17/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple must modify its App Tracking Transparency \(ATT\) rules to ensure third-party consent pop-ups are neutral and non-discouraging, following a German regulator&\#x27;s antitrust decision. The ruling gives Apple four months to implement changes and a seven-year commitment. This ruling could reshape how app developers obtain user consent for tracking, potentially increasing opt-in rates for third-party apps and leveling the playing field with Apple&\#x27;s own advertising. It also sets a precedent for antitrust scrutiny of platform consent mechanisms. Apple must remove discouraging wording and symbols from third-party ATT prompts, and the changes apply to both iPhone and iPad. The commitment lasts seven years, and Apple may appeal but the ruling is binding.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 17, 12:50

**Background**: App Tracking Transparency \(ATT\) is a feature introduced in iOS 14.5 that requires apps to get user permission before tracking them across other apps and websites, using the Identifier for Advertisers \(IDFA\). The German regulator found that Apple&\#x27;s implementation gave its own apps an advantage by using less intrusive prompts. Dark patterns are design elements that nudge users towards certain choices, and the ruling targets such patterns in third-party consent pop-ups.
