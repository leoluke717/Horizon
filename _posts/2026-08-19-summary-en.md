## [China and Australia Expand Local Currency Swap Agreement](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-19/10680599.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The People&\#x27;s Bank of China and the Reserve Bank of Australia renewed and expanded their bilateral local currency swap agreement, increasing the swap capacity from 200 billion RMB/41 billion AUD to 220 billion RMB/46 billion AUD, with a five-year term. This renewal and expansion facilitates bilateral trade and investment by reducing reliance on third-party currencies, supports financial stability, and reflects deepening financial cooperation between China and Australia. It also contributes to the internationalization of the renminbi. The agreement has a five-year term and can be extended by mutual consent. It sets a maximum swap capacity, not a guaranteed transaction, so actual usage may vary.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 19, 12:30

**Background**: A bilateral local currency swap is an agreement between two central banks to exchange their currencies up to a certain limit, providing liquidity and facilitating trade settlement without relying on a third currency like the US dollar. As of September 2025, the People&\#x27;s Bank of China has signed effective bilateral local currency swap agreements with 32 countries and regions, with a total scale of about 4.5 trillion RMB. These agreements help reduce exchange rate risk and promote the use of local currencies in international trade and investment.

## [Chongqing Issues 332 Land-Use Permits via &\#x27;No-Application&\#x27; Reform](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-19/10680568.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 19, Chongqing&\#x27;s Municipal Bureau of Planning and Natural Resources announced that 332 land-use planning permits have been issued through the &\#x27;免申即领&\#x27; \(exemption from application, immediate receipt\) model since the start of 2026. The reform lets eligible applicants receive permits without filing an application. This streamlines a previously paperwork-heavy administrative approval process, saving time and cost for developers and landowners in Chongqing. As a local government reform, it could serve as a model for other regions seeking to improve the business environment and policy delivery. The &\#x27;免申即领&\#x27; model relies on data sharing and automatic system review to match eligible applicants, similar to the broader &\#x27;免申即享&\#x27; policy approach. The land-use planning permit is a legal prerequisite for using land under China&\#x27;s Urban and Rural Planning Law, so the reform touches a core step in project development.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 19, 11:26

**Background**: A land-use planning permit \(建设用地规划许可证\) is an administrative certificate issued by the urban-rural planning authority, confirming that a construction project&\#x27;s land use complies with territorial spatial planning; it is a mandatory prerequisite before land can be requisitioned or allocated. &\#x27;免申即享&\#x27; is a policy service model based on data sharing and big-data analysis in which eligible businesses or citizens receive benefits without applying or submitting materials, often described as &\#x27;policy finds people.&\#x27; Chongqing&\#x27;s &\#x27;免申即领&\#x27; applies this idea to the issuance of land-use planning permits.

## [LandSpace Successfully Recovers Zhuque-3 First Stage with Landing Legs](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=85134) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 19, LandSpace launched the second Zhuque-3 rocket, successfully delivering the Hongque-03 satellite into orbit and achieving China&\#x27;s first recovery of an orbital-class rocket first stage using landing legs, with a soft landing in Gansu. This milestone makes China the fourth entity globally to achieve orbital-class reusable rocket recovery, after SpaceX&\#x27;s Falcon 9, Blue Origin&\#x27;s New Glenn, and China&\#x27;s own Long March 10B, potentially reducing launch costs and advancing commercial space capabilities. The Zhuque-3 rocket is 66 meters tall, 4.5 meters in diameter, powered by nine TQ-12 liquid oxygen methane engines on the first stage and one TQ-15 engine on the second stage, with a landing method similar to Falcon 9.

rss · Solidot · Aug 19, 07:08

**Background**: Reusable rocket technology aims to reduce launch costs by recovering and reusing the most expensive part of the rocket, the first stage. The TQ-12 engine, developed by LandSpace, is a gas-generator cycle liquid oxygen methane engine with 67 tons of sea-level thrust. China previously achieved orbital-class recovery with the Long March 10B using a sea net system, while the Zhuque-3 uses landing legs like Falcon 9.

## [Apple Adjusts EU Alternative App Store Fees, Up to 20% Commission for Alternative Payments](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/apple-changes-fees-alternative-app-stores-eu-2026-08-18/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple announced a new fee structure for EU developers effective October 1st: a 5% core technology commission for apps distributed via alternative marketplaces or the web, and a 20% commission for apps using alternative payment within the App Store \(10% for small businesses\). This replaces the previous acquisition and store service fees. This change is a direct response to the EU Digital Markets Act, aiming to comply with regulations that require fair competition and open platforms. It affects all EU developers and alternative app stores, potentially lowering costs for some while introducing new fees for others. The new fee structure eliminates the previous acquisition fee and store service fee. The 5% core technology commission applies to digital transactions for apps distributed outside the App Store, while the 20% commission applies to apps using alternative payment within the App Store.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 19, 01:19

**Background**: The EU Digital Markets Act \(DMA\) is a regulation aimed at curbing anti-competitive practices by large tech platforms, requiring them to allow alternative app stores and payment systems. Apple has been under scrutiny for its App Store policies, and this fee adjustment is part of ongoing negotiations with the European Commission. The DMA has already led to other changes, such as allowing sideloading and alternative payment methods.
