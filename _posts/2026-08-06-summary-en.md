## [China Revises Accounting Standard No. 30 on Financial Statement Presentation](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/08-05/10672578.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Ministry of Finance of China has revised and issued Accounting Standard No. 30 on Financial Statement Presentation, introducing an optimized profit statement structure, mandatory disclosure of management performance indicators, clarified aggregation and disaggregation principles, and a refined definition of materiality. The revision focuses on presentation and disclosure improvements without altering recognition and measurement requirements. This revision enhances the transparency and comparability of financial statements for enterprises applying Chinese Accounting Standards, benefiting investors, auditors, and other users. It aligns Chinese standards more closely with international practices by requiring management performance indicators disclosure. The new standard requires that if an enterprise uses a non-GAAP measure of profit or loss in publicly available documents, it must disclose the measure and reconcile it to the nearest GAAP measure. The definition of materiality has been refined to align with the concept that information is material if omitting, misstating, or obscuring it could reasonably be expected to influence decisions of primary users.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 5, 13:09

**Background**: Accounting Standard No. 30 originally issued in 2006 governs the presentation of financial statements for Chinese enterprises. The revision aims to improve the quality and transparency of financial reporting, responding to market developments and international convergence. The standard applies to all enterprises that adopt Chinese Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises \(ASBE\).

## [Chinese Exchanges Shut LAN Lines, Triggering Server Relocation and Rent Surge](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/lH2IAcm1uX33Hw1H_EfPDg) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Beijing stock exchanges announced the closure of local area network \(LAN\) trading and market data lines, requiring institutional traders to switch to wide area network \(WAN\) connections with a minimum round-trip latency of 2 milliseconds, forcing servers to be relocated out of exchange data centers. This has led to a sharp increase in rents for nearby financial-grade machine rooms, with standard 4000-watt cabinets in the Jinqiao area rising from about 7,000 yuan to around 10,000 yuan per month. This regulatory change eliminates the proximity advantage for high-frequency traders who relied on ultra-low latency connections, leveling the playing field but also increasing infrastructure costs for all institutional traders. It signals a broader push by Chinese regulators to reduce the perceived unfair advantages of high-frequency trading and ensure market fairness. The deadline for the LAN closure is reportedly July 31, 2026 for trading lines, while market data lines may close earlier; the new WAN requirement mandates a minimum 2ms round-trip latency, effectively preventing any server from being placed too close to the exchange. The rent surge is most acute in the Jinqiao area, where prime locations have seen cabinet rents double from earlier this year.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 5, 14:44

**Background**: Chinese stock exchanges operate on a &\#x27;price priority, time priority&\#x27; matching principle, meaning orders that reach the exchange&\#x27;s matching engine first are executed first. To gain speed advantages, many institutional traders, especially high-frequency trading firms, used direct market access \(DMA\) or co-location services to place their servers inside or very close to exchange data centers, achieving extremely low latency. The closure of LAN lines and the mandatory 2ms latency floor effectively ends this practice, forcing all traders to connect via WAN links with a minimum delay.
