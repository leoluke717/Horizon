## [FDA Approves First-in-Class KRAS-Targeted Therapy for Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-class-targeted-therapy-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The FDA approved RMC-6236, a first-in-class RAS\(ON\) multi-selective inhibitor, for adults with KRAS-mutant metastatic pancreatic cancer. The approval came just over a month after the agency accepted the new drug application, under the FDA&\#x27;s CNPV pilot program. KRAS has been considered &\#x27;undruggable&\#x27; for more than 30 years, so this approval opens a new treatment class for one of the deadliest cancers. It also sets a regulatory precedent for targeting RAS mutations and is likely to expand to other KRAS-mutant cancers. RMC-6236 is a RAS\(ON\) multi-selective inhibitor that binds the active, GTP-bound form of mutant RAS, rather than a single mutation such as G12C. The approval was completed in just over a month from NDA acceptance under the FDA&\#x27;s CNPV pilot program, compared with typical review timelines of 8-12 months.

hackernews · leopoldj · Aug 26, 16:19 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451675)

**Background**: KRAS is the most frequently mutated oncogene in human cancers, but for decades it resisted drug development because its protein surface lacked obvious binding pockets. Earlier KRAS inhibitors such as sotorasib and adagrasib target only the G12C mutation by covalently binding a cysteine residue. RMC-6236 belongs to a newer class that inhibits multiple RAS mutations, which is especially relevant for pancreatic cancer, where KRAS mutations occur in the vast majority of tumors.
