## [China&\#x27;s 15th Five-Year Plan Sets Binding 2030 Ecological Targets](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2026/08-10/10675453.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China&\#x27;s State Council released the Ecological Protection 15th Five-Year Plan on August 10, 2026, establishing binding 2030 targets including protected area coverage ≥18% of land area, ecological quality index of 86.5, soil conservation rate ≥74%, and forest coverage of 25.8%. This plan marks a structural shift in China&\#x27;s environmental regulation by setting legally binding quantitative targets for 2030, which will drive coordinated action across government agencies, local governments, and industries while signaling China&\#x27;s commitment to ecological civilization. The plan sets four key binding targets for 2030: protected area coverage ≥18% of land area, Ecological Quality Index \(EQI\) of 86.5, soil and water conservation rate ≥74%, and forest coverage 25.8%. Specific enforcement mechanisms and funding allocations have not been disclosed in the announcement.

This development enhances the region&\#x27;s trade infrastructure, potentially boosting local economic growth and attracting foreign investment. It streamlines customs procedures for businesses, reducing costs and improving efficiency in import/export activities. rss · 中国新闻网 - 财经 · Aug 10, 13:50

**Background**: A comprehensive bonded zone is a special area within a country that is treated as being outside its customs territory for tariff purposes. Goods can be stored, processed, or manufactured within the zone without being subject to customs duties until they enter the domestic market. Such zones are designed to promote trade, attract foreign investment, and support export-oriented industries. The Nanchang Comprehensive Bonded Zone was established to boost economic development in Jiangxi Province.
