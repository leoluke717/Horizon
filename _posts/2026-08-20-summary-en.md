## [South Korea Passes Law to Confiscate Illegal Gains, Targeting Chun Doo-hwan](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gj/2026/08-20/10681213.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The South Korean National Assembly passed a revision to the Act on Regulation and Punishment of Concealment of Criminal Proceeds, introducing an independent confiscation system that allows the state to recover criminal proceeds even without a criminal conviction. This law enables the recovery of illicit assets from powerful figures like former President Chun Doo-hwan, who amassed huge slush funds during his rule. It strengthens South Korea&\#x27;s ability to combat corruption and financial crimes. The independent confiscation system allows the state to seize assets derived from crime even if the perpetrator is deceased or has fled. The amendment passed with bipartisan support in the National Assembly.

rss · 中国新闻网 - 国际 · Aug 20, 10:30

**Background**: South Korea has long struggled to recover illicit funds from former authoritarian leaders. Chun Doo-hwan, who ruled in the 1980s, was convicted of treason and corruption but many of his illegal assets remained untouchable. The previous law required a criminal conviction to confiscate proceeds, which made it difficult to recover assets when the offender was deceased or immune from prosecution. The new independent confiscation system removes that requirement.

## [Amazon Rolls Out New KFX-ZIP Encryption on Kindle Oasis, Blocking Backup Tools](https://goodereader.com/blog/kindle/amazon-kindle-oasis-now-has-new-encryption-system) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Amazon has deployed a new KFX-ZIP encryption system on Kindle Oasis devices running firmware 5.18.2.1.1, and earlier on older Kindles with firmware 5.18.5 or newer. This change blocks third-party tools like Calibre and DeDRM from removing DRM for personal backup and format conversion. This change tightens Amazon&\#x27;s control over Kindle ebooks and affects users who rely on Calibre and DeDRM to back up or convert purchased books. It signals a continued crackdown on DRM removal across the Kindle ecosystem, leaving only Kindle Voyage users unaffected for now. The new encryption uses the KFX-ZIP format, an archive container that wraps KFX ebooks and requires the KFX Input Calibre plugin to unpack into a standard KFX file after decryption. Normal reading and downloading on Oasis remain unaffected; only third-party backup and conversion workflows are blocked.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 20, 01:37

**Background**: DRM \(Digital Rights Management\) uses encryption to tie purchased ebooks to specific devices or software, preventing format conversion and limiting which stores users can use. Calibre is a popular open-source ebook management tool, and DeDRM is a plugin set that removes DRM so users can convert and back up their books. Amazon&\#x27;s shift to KFX-ZIP encryption makes these tools harder to use, continuing the ongoing struggle between Amazon and DRM-removal tool developers.

## [China Customs Implements &\#x27;One Declaration, One Document&\#x27; for Sea-Rail Intermodal](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMigwFBVV95cUxPMjkwcktuN05UcEU4bUZmX09wSTl4Mk4wWFVjMEkta2R1Ujd4dzdWYXlGaWhnWWZQcHpzcnZrM2RxOW9tUHQ0bVNnX0MzRG15VEJrcHpPRmlpS290b2h0NFVyYnNqLUpjeVpERVpoQkYzaTZXeEJWTE9yS0ljQXN3eUxpdw?oc=5) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China Customs has launched a new supervision model for sea-rail intermodal transport that enables &\#x27;one declaration, one document through&\#x27; \(一次申报，一单贯通\), allowing exporters to complete customs clearance with a single declaration for the entire multimodal journey. This significantly reduces logistics costs and clearance time for export enterprises, especially those in inland regions using sea-rail intermodal transport, and enhances trade efficiency and competitiveness by simplifying multimodal customs procedures. The model has been piloted in locations including Shanghai, Fujian, and Fuzhou following the General Administration of Customs announcement \(No. 12 of 2026\), and early reports indicate domestic transport logistics costs can be reduced by 10% to 15%.

google\_news · customs.gov.cn · Aug 20, 08:50

**Background**: Sea-rail intermodal transport combines railway and sea shipping for import/export goods, allowing cargo to move from inland to ports via rail and then by ship. Traditionally, separate customs declarations were needed for each leg, causing delays. The new model unifies the process under a single declaration and continuous supervision from origin to port, eliminating redundant procedures.
