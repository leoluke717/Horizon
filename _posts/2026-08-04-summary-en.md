## [California Residents Can Request Data Brokers Delete Personal Data](https://www.solidot.org/story?sid=84991) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Starting August 1, 2025, California residents can use the Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform \(DROP\) to submit a single request to have their personal information deleted by hundreds of data brokers, replacing the previous process of contacting each broker individually. This new regulation simplifies the data deletion process for consumers, giving them more control over their personal data and setting a precedent for other states to follow in data privacy regulation. Data brokers must delete the user&\#x27;s data within 90 days of receiving the request, and subsequently delete it every 45 days. California is the first state to create an easy-to-use data deletion system.

rss · Solidot · Aug 3, 09:35

**Background**: Data brokers are companies that collect personal information from consumers without a direct relationship and sell it to third parties. The California Consumer Privacy Act \(CCPA\) defines data brokers and regulates their activities. Previously, consumers had to contact each broker individually to request deletion, which was cumbersome. The DROP platform streamlines this process by allowing a single request to multiple brokers.

The yen&\#x27;s decline is driven by interest rate differentials between Japan and the US, with the Bank of Japan maintaining low rates while the Federal Reserve has raised rates.
