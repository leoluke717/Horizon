## [Kimi K3: Open-Weight Frontier Model with Novel Attention and Expert Balancing](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vaysjf/how_kimi_k3_engineered_its_way_to_the_frontier_r/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, an open-weight model that ranks fourth among 580 models on Artificial Analysis, behind only Claude Opus 5, Fable 5, and GPT-5.6 Sol. It introduces Kimi Delta Attention, which reduces KV cache memory from 104.6 GiB to 27.2 GiB for a 1M-token context, Quantile Balancing for expert load balancing, and AgentENV, a microVM runtime for efficient reinforcement learning training. Kimi K3 advances open-weight frontier models by making large context windows more memory-efficient and improving training stability for mixture-of-experts architectures. Its techniques could lower the barrier for researchers and practitioners to train and deploy large models with extended contexts and agentic capabilities. Kimi Delta Attention replaces the KV cache in 69 of the 93 layers with a single 128x128 matrix per head. Quantile Balancing computes the bias directly from router score margins in one batch, unlike DeepSeek-V3&\#x27;s fixed-step bias nudging which breaks at 896 experts. AgentENV created 51 million sandboxes with 133 ms checkpoint and 49 ms resume times, allowing trajectories to pause for free during model thinking.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/noninertialframe96 · Jul 30, 16:37

**Background**: In transformer-based large language models, the KV cache stores key-value pairs from previous tokens to speed up generation, but its memory grows linearly with context length, making long contexts expensive. Mixture-of-Experts \(MoE\) models use multiple expert sub-networks per layer and require load balancing to ensure all experts are utilized evenly; traditional methods like auxiliary losses or fixed bias nudging can struggle at scale. Reinforcement learning for language models often involves running many agent-environment interactions in parallel, which demands efficient sandboxing and fast state saving. Kimi K3 addresses these challenges with Kimi Delta Attention \(a linear attention variant\), Quantile Balancing \(a direct bias computation method\), and AgentENV \(a Firecracker-based microVM runtime\).
